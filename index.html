01 / About

A designer who codes,
a student who's hungry.

I got into design the way most people do — by noticing things that weren't quite right. Misaligned buttons, cramped text, forms that made me sigh. Eventually I stopped complaining and opened Figma.

These days I split my time between designing interfaces, building them for the web, and testing them for QA. I like clean grids, generous whitespace, and type that actually wants to be read. My current focus is getting comfortable with React, and Python is next.

Still learning. Still broke. Still hungry — that's the whole vibe.

Thanks for stopping by. If something here made you smile or think, I'd love to hear about it. —
